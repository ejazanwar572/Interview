# 📖 Retail Definitions & Glossary

> Covers **Grocery / FMCG** and **Fashion / Apparel** sub-segments. Terms are grouped by domain for fast lookup.

---

## 1. Store Operations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **SKU** (Stock Keeping Unit) | A unique identifier for a product/variant (size, color, flavor). The atom of retail inventory. |
| **Planogram** | A visual diagram that specifies how products should be arranged on shelves to maximize sales. |
| **Shrinkage** | Inventory loss due to theft, spoilage, admin errors, or supplier fraud. Formula: `(Recorded Inventory − Actual Inventory) / Recorded Inventory`. |
| **Stockout** | When a product is unavailable for purchase (lost sales event). |
| **Phantom Inventory** | System records show stock, but the physical shelf is empty. Common in grocery. |
| **Markdown** | A permanent price reduction, typically used in fashion to clear end-of-season stock. |
| **Shrinkage Rate** | % of inventory "lost." Grocery benchmark: ~1–2%. Fashion: ~1.5–2.5%. |
| **Dead Stock** | Inventory that hasn't sold within a defined period and is unlikely to sell at full price. |
| **On-Shelf Availability (OSA)** | % of time a product is physically available on shelf when it should be. Target: >95%. |
| **BOPIS** | Buy Online, Pick-up In Store. Omnichannel fulfilment method. |
| **Click & Collect** | Synonym for BOPIS in UK/European retail. |
| **Last-Mile Delivery** | Final step of the product journey from distribution center to the customer's door. High-cost, high-impact. |

## 2. Product & Merchandising

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Category Management** | Process of managing product categories as strategic business units to maximize profitability. |
| **Assortment** | The collection of SKUs a retailer carries in a category or store. |
| **Core Range** | The always-on, must-stock set of SKUs (typically 20% of SKUs driving 80% of sales). |
| **Tail SKU** | Low-volume SKUs at the long tail of the assortment. Often candidates for delisting. |
| **Private Label / Store Brand** | Products manufactured for and sold under the retailer's own brand. Higher margin, differentiation tool. |
| **Cross-Merchandising** | Placing complementary products together (e.g., pasta + pasta sauce) to drive basket size. |
| **Cannibalization** | When a new product reduces sales of an existing product within the same category. |
| **Range Review** | Periodic assessment of which SKUs to add, keep, or delist. Driven by sell-through, margin, and space. |
| **Size Run** | *(Fashion)* The distribution of sizes stocked per style (e.g., XS: 5%, S: 20%, M: 35%, L: 30%, XL: 10%). |
| **OTB** (Open-to-Buy) | *(Fashion)* A budget that controls how much a buyer can spend on merchandise for a future period. |

## 3. Customer Analytics

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **CLV / LTV** | Customer Lifetime Value. Total revenue expected from a customer over their entire relationship. Formula: `CLV = ARPU × Gross Margin × Average Customer Lifespan`. |
| **CAC** | Customer Acquisition Cost. `CAC = Total Marketing & Sales Spend / Number of New Customers Acquired`. |
| **Churn Rate** | % of customers who stop purchasing within a defined period. `Churn = (Lost Customers / Customers at Start) × 100`. |
| **Retention Rate** | `1 − Churn Rate`. % of customers who continue buying. |
| **NPS** | Net Promoter Score. `NPS = % Promoters − % Detractors`. Scale: −100 to +100. Retail benchmark: 30–50+. |
| **RFM Model** | Segments customers by **R**ecency, **F**requency, **M**onetary value. Core tool for loyalty targeting. |
| **Cohort Analysis** | Tracking groups of customers (cohorts) who share a common characteristic (e.g., acquired in Jan) over time. |
| **Basket Analysis / MBA** | Market Basket Analysis. Identifies which products are frequently bought together (association rules). |
| **Share of Wallet (SOW)** | % of a customer's total spend in a category that goes to your store vs. competitors. |
| **Customer Segment** | A group of customers with similar behavior, preferences, or demographics. |
| **First-Party Data** | Data collected directly from customers via loyalty programs, apps, or owned channels. Gold standard for personalization. |
| **CSAT** | Customer Satisfaction Score. Point-in-time satisfaction rating (1–5 or 1–10 scale). |

## 4. Sales & Revenue

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **GMV** | Gross Merchandise Value. Total value of goods sold before deducting returns or fees. |
| **Net Revenue** | Revenue after returns, discounts, and allowances. |
| **ATV / AOV** | Average Transaction Value / Average Order Value. `ATV = Total Revenue / Number of Transactions`. |
| **Conversion Rate** | `Conversions / Total Visitors × 100`. In-store: transactions / footfall. Online: purchases / sessions. |
| **Sales per Square Foot** | `Total Revenue / Retail Floor Space (sq ft)`. Efficiency of space utilization. Grocery benchmark: $400–$600/sqft/yr. |
| **Comp-Store Sales** | Same-store sales growth: revenue change for stores open ≥12 months. Removes new store effect. |
| **Sell-Through Rate** | `Units Sold / Units Received × 100`. Tracks how fast inventory moves. Fashion target: >70% at full price. |
| **Lift** | Incremental sales driven by a promotion vs. the baseline. `Lift = (Promo Sales − Baseline) / Baseline`. |
| **Basket Size** | Number of items per transaction. |
| **Basket Value** | Total spend per transaction (= ATV). |
| **Cross-sell / Upsell Rate** | % of customers who purchase an add-on or higher-tier product. |
| **YoY Growth** | `(Current Period Sales − Prior Year Same Period) / Prior Year Same Period × 100`. |

## 5. Inventory & Supply Chain

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **COGS** | Cost of Goods Sold. Direct cost of producing the goods sold. |
| **Gross Margin** | `(Revenue − COGS) / Revenue × 100`. Fashion runs ~50–60%; Grocery ~25–30%. |
| **Inventory Turnover** | `COGS / Average Inventory`. How many times inventory is sold/replaced per year. Grocery: 10–20×; Fashion: 4–6×. |
| **DIO** | Days Inventory Outstanding. `365 / Inventory Turnover`. Grocery: 18–36 days; Fashion: 60–90 days. |
| **Safety Stock** | Buffer inventory held to prevent stockouts from demand/supply variability. |
| **Reorder Point (ROP)** | Inventory level that triggers a new purchase order. `ROP = Lead Time Demand + Safety Stock`. |
| **Lead Time** | Time from placing an order to receiving goods. |
| **Fill Rate** | % of customer orders fulfilled from available stock without backorders. |
| **Markdown %** | `(Original Price − Marked-Down Price) / Original Price × 100`. |
| **Overstock / Excess Inventory** | Inventory beyond what can be sold at full price within a season. A major fashion profitability risk. |

## 6. Digital & Omnichannel

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Omnichannel** | Integrated customer experience across all touchpoints (store, app, web, social). |
| **Attribution Model** | Method for assigning credit to marketing touchpoints leading to a conversion (first-touch, last-touch, linear, data-driven). |
| **Cart Abandonment Rate** | `(Carts Created − Completed Purchases) / Carts Created × 100`. Industry avg: ~70%. |
| **Bounce Rate** | % of visitors who leave after viewing only one page. |
| **ROAS** | Return on Ad Spend. `Revenue from Ads / Ad Spend`. Retail benchmark: 4× or higher. |
| **CPA** | Cost Per Acquisition. `Ad Spend / Number of New Customers`. |
| **A/B Test** | Controlled experiment comparing two variants (A = control, B = treatment) to measure causal impact. |
| **Incrementality Test** | Measures true lift from a campaign by isolating causal effect (holdout group method). |
| **Personalization Engine** | Algorithmic system that tailors product recommendations, content, or pricing to individual users. |

## 7. Analytics Types (Ladder)

| Type | Question Answered | Example in Retail |
|---|---|---|
| **Descriptive** | What happened? | Weekly sales dashboard, YoY trends |
| **Diagnostic** | Why did it happen? | Root cause of sales decline |
| **Predictive** | What will happen? | Demand forecasting, churn prediction |
| **Prescriptive** | What should we do? | Price optimization, stock allocation |

## 8. Grocery-Specific Terms

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **FMCG** | Fast Moving Consumer Goods. High-volume, low-cost goods (food, beverages, household). |
| **Fresh / Perish** | Refers to fresh produce, dairy, meat — high shrinkage risk; short shelf life. |
| **Replenishment Cycle** | How frequently stock is ordered and restocked (daily for fresh, weekly for ambient). |
| **Waste %** | % of fresh inventory expired/discarded. Key P&L lever for grocery. |
| **Basket Penetration** | % of baskets containing a given product in a given period. |
| **Loyalty Card Data** | Rich longitudinal customer-level purchase history. Primary data asset for grocery analytics. |
| **Promotional Compliance** | % of stores correctly executing a planned promotion (right price, right placement). |

---

## 9. Fashion / Apparel-Specific Terms

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Season** | Fashion production/sales cycle: Spring-Summer (SS), Autumn-Winter (AW). Drives markdown timing. |
| **Carryover SKU** | Style/SKU that continues across seasons (e.g., basic white tee). Lower markdown risk. |
| **In-season vs. Off-price** | In-season = full price; off-price = discounted (outlets, clearance). |
| **Return Rate** | *(Fashion)* Typically 20–40% in e-commerce due to sizing. A major margin eroder. |
| **NDC** | National Distribution Centre. Central warehouse distributing to stores. |
| **Visual Merchandising** | Presentation of products in-store to maximize appeal and sales (equivalent to planogram). |
| **OTIF** | On-Time In-Full. % of orders delivered on time and in full quantity. Supply chain health KPI. |
| **White Space** | Opportunity to introduce a new product/style where there is unmet customer demand. |
